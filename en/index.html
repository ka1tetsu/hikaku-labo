Systeme.io vs Thinkific: Which Is Better for Selling Your First Online Course?
One is an all-in-one platform with a free plan that can actually take payments. The other is a dedicated course platform with no free plan but a deeper learning feature set. We compare current pricing, plan limits and per-sale fees so you can pick based on your situation, not on marketing.

What we compare

  • Online course platforms: what a first course actually costs, including the fees taken from each sale.
  • Email marketing: contact limits, automation depth and what happens to the price as a list grows.
  • Hosting for course and content sites: managed vs. shared hosting, priced on published plans.
  • Productivity and business tools: the software a one-person business runs on.

How we compare

  • Prices and plan limits are taken from each provider's official pages, and every article states the date they were checked. Prices change; we link to the source so you can confirm before you buy.
  • We compare published specifications. Where we have not used a feature ourselves, the article says so instead of dressing up a spec sheet as a hands-on review.
  • Affiliate commissions never decide the order or the outcome of a comparison. See the disclosure on each article and our editorial policy.
