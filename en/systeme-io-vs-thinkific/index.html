Systeme.io vs Thinkific: Which Is Better for Selling Your First Online Course?

Systeme.io is an all-in-one marketing platform whose free plan can host a course and take payments. Thinkific is a platform built specifically for learning products, but it has no free plan: paid plans start at $109 a month. For a first course, the right choice depends on whether you need to validate the idea cheaply or build a course business on dedicated ground.

The short answer

Choose Systeme.io if this is your first course, your audience is small or nonexistent, and you want to find out whether anyone will pay before you commit to a monthly bill. Its free plan includes one course, an email list of up to 2,000 contacts and checkout with no platform transaction fee: enough to make real sales at $0 a month.

Choose Thinkific if the course itself is the long-term product and you expect to use learning-specific features (communities, memberships, digital downloads, coaching) from the start, and if paying $82–$109 a month before your first sale is acceptable. There is no free plan, only a 30-day trial, so the meter starts running quickly.

Fees taken from each sale

Systeme.io charges 0% platform transaction fees on every plan, including the free plan. You pay only your payment processor's standard fees through its Stripe and PayPal integrations. For a low-priced first course, this is the difference between keeping and losing a noticeable slice of each sale.

Thinkific also charges no additional platform fee when you sell through Thinkific Payments (its built-in processor, TCommerce); its pricing FAQ puts the standard processing rate at 2.9%, in line with Stripe and PayPal. If you instead connect your own Stripe or PayPal account, Thinkific adds a third-party gateway fee that varies by plan. Check the pricing page for your tier.

Beyond the course: what else is in the box

Systeme.io: the all-in-one trade-off

One subscription covers funnels, email campaigns and automation, a blog, course hosting and even running your own affiliate program: the tools a first-time seller would otherwise have to buy and connect separately. The trade-off is depth: the course player is one module in a marketing platform, and the free plan's limits (1 course, 1 automation rule, 500 students, per the official FAQ) are set for validation, not for a catalogue.

Thinkific: the specialist trade-off

Everything is organised around learning products: course building, communities, memberships, downloads, coaching and live sessions, with student management and progress tracking. The trade-offs are the missing surrounding toolkit (email marketing and funnels are not what the plans are built around) and the price floor. (Some enterprise features, such as SCORM, are reserved for the custom-priced Plus plan.)

Who Systeme.io is for (and who should skip it)

A good fit if you:

  • are selling your first course and cannot yet justify $100+/month in platform costs;
  • have no email tool yet and want the list, the funnel and the course in one bill;
  • sell at a low price point, where a platform transaction fee would hurt;
  • want a free plan that can take real payments, not a 14-day countdown.

Think twice if you:

  • plan a catalogue of many courses with cohorts, assignments and detailed learner tracking as the core experience;
  • expect to pass 500 students quickly: you will be pushed up the plan ladder;
  • need SCORM or enterprise training features.

Who Thinkific is for (and who should skip it)

A good fit if you:

  • are building a course business, academy or training offer as a long-term product;
  • want communities, memberships and downloads next to your courses from day one;
  • expect thousands of students and prefer a high included cap (10,000) over a low entry price;
  • already have an email platform and do not need an all-in-one.

Think twice if you:

  • have not validated the course idea yet: $109/month (or $82 on annual billing) is a real cost before the first sale, and the 30-day trial is the only free runway;
  • need funnels and email automation included rather than integrated;
  • are price-testing a small course for a small audience, where the plan cost dwarfs early revenue.

Bottom line

For a first course, the deciding fact is simple: Systeme.io lets you sell before you spend, and Thinkific asks you to spend before you sell. Start on Systeme.io's free plan if you are validating; move to Thinkific when the course is proven and its learning-specific features (communities, memberships, a 10,000-student cap) are worth $82–$109 a month to you. Neither choice is permanent: both platforms let you export your customer list, so the cost of switching later is lower than the cost of overpaying now.
