# PRODUCT — Vivlica

Vivlica (https://vivlica.com) is a Japanese-language product and service comparison website, developed as React/Vite frontend with Vercel APIs in ka1tetsu/hikaku-labo.

## Visitor jobs
- Compare actual product and store prices, knowing the source and freshness limitations.
- Estimate recurring expenses; compare mobile phone contracts, broadband, cards and insurance.
- Select IT tools by budget and intended use.
- Estimate total trip cost and inspect reservation options and caveats.
- Read original guidance, method disclosures, and affiliate notices.

## Trust constraints
Never invent price data, merchant availability, rankings, user testimonials, partnerships, or revenue. Preserve existing affiliate query parameters, analytics, advertising notices, comparison methodology, forms, APIs and pricing caveats. A design-only change cannot certify conversions or production deployments.

## Desired experience
Readability and factual clarity first, especially on mobile. Make differences, totals, dates, methods and next actions readily scannable, accessible with keyboard and reduced motion.
