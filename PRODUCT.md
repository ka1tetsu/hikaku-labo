# Vivlica — product truth

## Who this is for
People in Japan comparing recurring costs, physical products, software tools, flights/hotels, and public-company information. They want to make a purchase or research decision without confusing estimated totals, outdated data, marketing content, or affiliate commissions with verified facts.

## What we actually provide
- **Shopping:** prices/items received from approved shopping APIs and separate onward search links. The displayed product price is not a guaranteed all-merchant checkout total; unknown delivery costs remain unknown.
- **Life:** user-configured mobile/internet/card comparisons based on manually checked official plan descriptions. Discounts, address eligibility, devices, and termination fees may not be included.
- **Tools:** a rule-based fit score over a small curated catalog with official source links and disclosed limitations; not verified buyer satisfaction or AI objective rankings.
- **Travel:** destination suggestions from a curated catalog, approved booking redirects, Yahoo! Travel A8 creative where permitted, Rakuten hotel listings if the access key and upstream service work, and voluntary user-entered all-in cost comparisons. An A8 banner does **not** supply a live hotel rate. A hotel's first-night guide price is not a multi-night checkout total.
- **Database:** link to a separately operated corporate data product; this comparison site does not assert real-time completeness or investment recommendations.

## What is not currently established
- No comprehensive inventory of all online stores or global hotels.
- No automatic real-time total-price comparison of every booking provider.
- No first-hand product testing unless the article cites actual tested material.
- No independent confirmation of any affiliate commission or profit solely from links.
- The site concierge is a **local rules-and-data assistant**, not an external reasoning LLM. When it retrieves product search results those queries may reach the shopping API.

## Editorial promise
**Evidence before polish.** We show what was measured, derived, or manually entered; the conditions and exclusions; the source and its date where available; and a user-actionable next step. Never generate first-person testing anecdotes, fake user endorsements, plausible-but-unverified prices, or claims of exhaustive research.

## Success measures
A user can answer: "Which compared option is better for my specific conditions, why, what is missing, and where is the original source?" Measure valid comparison completions and outbound clicks separately from verified ASP confirmations and net profit.

## Constraints
- Japanese-first and mobile-first, accessible with keyboard and reduced motion.
- Keep previously published routes, SEO canonicals, and A8 ad snippets verbatim.
- Do not ship expensive generative APIs or unlicensed catalogs.
- Do not collect user-entered prices or travel conditions in analytics.
- Run npm test/lint/build/verify and the Impeccable detector before merging public UI changes.
